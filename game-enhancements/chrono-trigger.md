# Chrono Trigger

| --- | --- | --- |
| HD pack (sprites) | Character sprites, menus, effects, world map objects (260 MB ASTC zip). Field and town backgrounds are not covered yet: their layouts come from SNES-style chip and map tables. Most field NPCs stay native (the game picks their palettes per NPC). Recipe: [YQUE](../tools/hd_remaster/games/YQUE/README.md) | Title (logo sprites 2580/2640); Crono's room |
| 3D Crono (test) | Crono's standing, walking and running frames rendered from a rigged 3D model in a Toriyama cel-shaded style (`chara3d.py`) | Crono's room, walking in four directions |
